Four new iBlock space sets build a connected visual language around white, grey, black, and orange models. The group moves from a tower-crane base to a large rover, a streamlined spacecraft, and a smaller mining scene. Together they offer several ways to build around vehicles, equipment, and exploration-focused display subjects.

## A base and rover with a tall working silhouette

The 1,000-piece [iBlock Galactic Tower Crane Base and Rover Building Set](/products/galactic-tower-crane-base-and-rover-building-set), SKU LL001, combines two finished models. The stated crane-base size is 27.6 × 29.1 × 16.3 cm, while the rover measures 11.4 × 9.3 × 7.5 cm. The public image shows a tall orange crane structure mounted on a white base, alongside a compact tracked rover and astronaut figures.

![White and orange brick rover with six wheels and an articulated grabbing arm](/images/blog/new-releases/new-iblock-space-ll001-ll002-ll003-ll004-body-01.avif "The LL002 rover uses six large wheels, an extended arm, and a compact cockpit.")

## A large rover built around wheels and reach

SKU LL002, the [iBlock Robotic Arm Rover Building Set](/products/robotic-arm-rover-building-set), contains 643 pieces and has a stated finished size of 24 × 14 × 14 cm. Its product image presents a six-wheel vehicle with an enclosed cockpit, orange wheel accents, equipment mounted around the body, and an extended grabbing arm.

## A spacecraft with a broad, low profile

The [iBlock Versatile Spacecraft Building Set](/products/versatile-spacecraft-building-set), SKU LL003, contains 944 pieces and measures 28.6 × 22.5 × 10.8 cm when finished. The public image shows a white spacecraft with a transparent cockpit, black wing sections, grey side panels, orange details, and a seated astronaut figure.

![White brick spacecraft with a clear cockpit black wings and orange accents](/images/blog/new-releases/new-iblock-space-ll001-ll002-ll003-ll004-body-02.avif "The LL003 spacecraft uses a wide wing shape and a low cockpit-led profile.")

## A multi-part mining scene

The 502-piece [iBlock Deep Space Mining Crew Building Set](/products/deep-space-mining-crew-building-set), SKU LL004, is presented as three finished elements: a mining robot, a control station, and a spacecraft. Their stated sizes are 7.64 × 13.5 × 7.09 cm, 10.09 × 8.79 × 6.4 cm, and 19.8 × 22.4 × 7.03 cm respectively.

![Brick space mining scene with spacecraft robot control station and astronaut](/images/blog/new-releases/new-iblock-space-ll001-ll002-ll003-ll004-body-03.avif "The LL004 public image groups its spacecraft, mining robot, control station, and figure.")

All four public records list a recommended age of 8+, while build difficulty is directed to the product package. Collectors can compare the larger single-model silhouettes with the smaller scene-based format and choose the subject that best fits their preferred shelf arrangement.
