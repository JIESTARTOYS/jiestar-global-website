Four very different building directions have recently been added to the JIESTAR online catalog. Two GULY mechanical models focus on engineering form and automotive display, while two JIESTAR botanical builds use bricks to interpret flowers, colour, and decorative composition. Together, they show how a building block collection can move from exposed machinery to art-inspired interior display without relying on a single theme.

“Recently added” refers to their appearance in the current online catalog. It is not a claim about a global launch date, stock position, sales performance, or availability in every market. Buyers should use the individual product pages for the current listing and contact JIESTAR when specifications or commercial terms need to be confirmed.

## GULY W16 Engine Model Kit 60556

The [GULY W16 Engine Model Kit 60556](/products/guly-w16-engine-model-kit-60556) presents an engine and transmission as the finished subject rather than hiding the mechanical system inside a vehicle body. The official product image shows a dense display model built around metallic-looking grey elements, visible pipework, transparent details, red highlights, and a black presentation base.

This direction may appeal to builders who enjoy mechanical subjects, automotive engineering, and models that invite close visual inspection after assembly. Its long horizontal form also gives it a different display profile from a car or building. On a desk, shelf, showroom counter, or enthusiast display, the model reads as a technical object rather than a conventional toy vehicle.

![GULY W16 engine building block display model 60556](/images/blog/new-releases/guly-w16-engine-60556-body.avif "The official catalog image for the GULY W16 Engine Model Kit 60556.")

## GULY 1:8 Remote-Control Drift Stunt Car Model Kit 10659

The [GULY 1:8 Remote-Control Drift Stunt Car Model Kit 10659](/products/guly-1-8-remote-control-drift-stunt-car-model-kit-10659) takes a more recognisable automotive route. Its official image shows a low red-and-black performance car with an open technical structure, prominent aerodynamic surfaces, wide wheels, and exposed building details.

The product title identifies the model as a remote-control drift stunt car kit. Buyers interested in its functions, included components, control system, power requirements, scale, or package contents should confirm those details on the current product listing or through a direct enquiry rather than inferring them from the photograph alone.

Visually, the car is suited to builders who want a dramatic automotive silhouette and a model that communicates motion even when parked. The contrast between the red bodywork, black structure, and visible technical elements makes it useful for display-led product photography as well as physical presentation.

![Red GULY remote-control drift stunt car building block model 10659](/images/blog/new-releases/guly-drift-car-10659-body.avif "The official catalog image for the GULY 1:8 Remote-Control Drift Stunt Car Model Kit 10659.")

## JIESTAR Impressionist Water Lily Pond Building Set

The [JIESTAR Impressionist Water Lily Pond Building Set](/products/x88059-jiestar-impressionist-water-lily-pond-building-set) moves from machinery to a framed botanical scene. The model combines water lilies, foliage, a curved bridge, decorative borders, and layered blue-and-green water details inside a vertical composition.

Unlike a freestanding bouquet, this set reads like a small three-dimensional artwork. The framing gives the build a defined front view, while the plants and bridge create depth across several layers. It may suit collectors looking for a decorative model for a bookcase, desk, studio, or living space, especially when a conventional vehicle or architecture model would feel too technical.

The “impressionist” wording describes the product direction shown in the official title and imagery. It should not be treated as a claim of affiliation with a particular artist or artwork unless explicitly stated in current product documentation.

![JIESTAR impressionist water lily pond building block scene X88059](/images/blog/new-releases/jiestar-water-lily-x88059-body.avif "The official catalog image for the JIESTAR Impressionist Water Lily Pond Building Set.")

## JIESTAR Wicked Beauty Flower Garden Building Set

The [JIESTAR Wicked Beauty Flower Garden Building Set](/products/x88057-jiestar-wicked-beauty-flower-garden-building-set) uses black calla-lily forms, deep red flowers, curved stems, and a black vase to create a darker botanical arrangement. The result is more sculptural than naturalistic, with a strong colour contrast that separates it from pastel or traditional bouquet sets.

This model may be relevant to collectors who prefer dramatic interior accents and compact display pieces. Its vertical silhouette can work in narrower spaces, while the repeated flower forms provide visual detail from more than one angle. The official image presents it as a complete arrangement, but current dimensions, piece count, recommended age, and packaging should be checked on the product page.
