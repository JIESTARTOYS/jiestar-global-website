Two new iBlock city sets bring food-truck themes into compact building scenes. The pair shares a familiar format—four wheels, a service counter, and an oversized roof sign—while using very different color palettes and food-inspired details.

The [iBlock Donut Cart Food Truck Building Set](/products/donut-cart-food-truck-building-set) is built around a blue cart with a striped awning and a large donut sign. Its published specification lists 458 pieces and a finished model size of 14.8 x 7.3 x 13.5 cm. The official product image also shows pastry details at the service window and a clear windshield at the front of the vehicle.

![Black brick-built burger diner truck with a large burger roof sign](/images/blog/new-releases/new-iblock-city-ll202-ll201-body-01.avif "The burger diner truck has a large burger sign and a detailed service counter.")

## A second stop for the city scene

The [iBlock Diner Rush Burger Food Truck Building Set](/products/diner-rush-burger-food-truck-building-set) uses a black, brown, green, and red palette, topped with a burger-shaped sign. Its published specification lists 473 pieces and a finished model size of 15.4 x 7.3 x 13.7 cm. The product imagery shows food-themed elements along the side counter, giving the model a distinct diner character.

Both sets are recommended for ages 8+ according to their public product details. Together, the donut cart and burger diner offer two different ways to build a small food-service street scene, whether displayed separately or placed side by side.
