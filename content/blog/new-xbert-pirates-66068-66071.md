## Two new coastal scenes from Xbert

Xbert adds two pirate-themed building sets to the New Releases selection: the Royal Fortress Building Set and the Imperial Outpost Building Set. Both official image sets centre on coastal structures, with docks, towers, waterline baseplates, and small scene details.

![Brick-built imperial outpost with tower and waterside dock](/images/blog/new-releases/new-xbert-pirates-66068-66071-body-01.avif)

## Royal Fortress Building Set

The [Xbert Royal Fortress Building Set](/products/xbert-66068-xbert-royal-fortress-building-set) is SKU 66068. It contains 5,069 pieces, is recommended for ages 14+, and has a finished model size of 45.4 × 45.9 × 40.3 cm. The official images show a multi-level fortress with a gatehouse, towers, a dock, and a blue water base.

![Large brick-built royal fortress on a blue water base](/images/blog/new-releases/new-xbert-pirates-66068-66071-body-02.avif)

## Imperial Outpost Building Set

The [Xbert Imperial Outpost Building Set](/products/xbert-66071-xbert-imperial-outpost-building-set) is SKU 66071. It contains 1,326 pieces, is recommended for ages 8+, and has a finished model size of 26.1 × 29.8 × 26.7 cm. Its official images show a smaller outpost scene with a tower, a dock, and coastal details.
