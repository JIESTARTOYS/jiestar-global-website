Three iBlock building sets arrive with different takes on flight-themed display scenes. The collection moves from a bright airport terminal and jet to two jungle-inspired builds, each with its own vehicle, setting, and small character details.

The [iBlock Dreamy Airport Jet Building Set](/products/dreamy-airport-jet-building-set) brings an airport and jet together in one scene. Its published specifications list 891 pieces, with a separate airport and jet model. The set is recommended for ages 8+, and the official product imagery highlights the white, pink, and transparent-blue palette across the terminal and aircraft.

![Jungle vehicle and helicopter building models on a white background](/images/blog/new-releases/new-iblock-aircraft-ll110-ll302-ll303-body-01.avif "The jungle vehicle and helicopter appear together with a small landscape build.")

## Jungle exploration from two directions

The [iBlock Jungle Off-Road Vehicle and Helicopter Building Set](/products/jungle-off-road-vehicle-and-helicopter-building-set) combines an off-road vehicle, helicopter, and a compact landscape element. It has a published piece count of 467 and is recommended for ages 8+. The product image shows a navy, white, and orange color scheme, along with a small bridge scene.

The [iBlock Jungle Seaplane Building Set](/products/jungle-seaplane-building-set) takes the same setting toward the water. Its published model list includes a seaplane and tree, with 483 pieces and an 8+ recommendation. The official image shows the seaplane alongside a dock, tree, and crocodile build, giving the set a distinct display silhouette.

![Blue and orange seaplane with a small tree and dock scene](/images/blog/new-releases/new-iblock-aircraft-ll110-ll302-ll303-body-02.avif "The seaplane build is shown beside a tree, dock, and crocodile scene.")
