JIESTAR exhibited at IBTE Vietnam 2025 with a focused building block presentation at booth B.D11. The event created a place for retailers, distributors, ecommerce businesses, and prospective development partners to view products in physical form and decide which directions warranted a more detailed conversation.

The image selected for this article visibly belongs to the Vietnam appearance. Similar files found in the Indonesia and Vietnam folders were not published twice; each fair story uses its own recognisable booth photograph.

## Verified event information

IBTE Vietnam 2025 was held from December 18 to 20 at the Saigon Exhibition and Convention Center in Ho Chi Minh City. The dates and venue are confirmed by the organiser's [official event information](https://www.ibtefair.com/NewsDetail/5632886.html), while the photographed booth signage shows B.D11.

## What the photograph records

The booth image shows JIESTAR branding above a presentation of boxed building block products and finished models. The arrangement gives visitors both a quick overview and a path to closer comparison. Package fronts communicate the intended product story, while assembled builds help buyers judge physical form, visual detail, and display value.

Multiple items are presented as a range rather than as isolated products. This is relevant for buyers who need an assortment that looks coherent across a store shelf, online collection page, or distributor catalogue. The photograph confirms the event presentation, but it does not verify present-day price, stock, specification, territory, or order terms.

## A useful review point for the Vietnamese market

Different channels may approach the same display with different priorities. A specialist toy retailer may consider building experience and model detail. A gift or lifestyle channel may focus on finished appearance and packaging. An ecommerce operator may look for strong visual storytelling, while a distributor must also think about range structure and repeat ordering.

At a fair meeting, buyers can identify the relevant product directions and then request the information required for local evaluation. This can include current specifications, packaging, testing documentation, minimum order quantity, quotation, production timing, and after-sales support. JIESTAR's [quality and safety page](/quality-safety) provides a starting framework for those questions.

## Choosing the right cooperation path

For current product selection and channel supply, buyers can begin with the [wholesale page](/wholesale). A useful wholesale brief should state the destination market, sales channel, preferred categories, approximate quantity range, and expected timing.

Partners who need an original concept, custom packaging, brand application, an exclusive SKU, or a longer-term product programme should use [Custom Solutions](/custom-solutions). A development discussion benefits from a target customer, reference direction, functional requirements, desired packaging, target timing, and an honest quantity estimate. These details help define feasibility; they do not guarantee a commercial result before review.
