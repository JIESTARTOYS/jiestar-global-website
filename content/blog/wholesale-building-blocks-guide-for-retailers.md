Wholesale building block buyers should begin with product category fit. Super cars, trains, architecture, military models, book nooks, and educational blocks can each serve different sales channels and customer groups.

Before placing an order, confirm the product catalog, MOQ, package details, expected lead time, shipping options, and after-sales support. These details help reduce risk when launching products in a new market.

Retailers and ecommerce sellers should also consider whether the supplier can provide new product updates and long-term product line support. A stable product roadmap helps channels plan seasonal launches and repeat orders.
