JIESTAR has added three railway-themed building sets for collectors who enjoy locomotive forms, trackside details, and display-focused scenes. The new group brings together the Fallen Steam Train X58001, Mallard Steam Train X58002, and German 103 Electric Locomotive X58003.

## A railway trio with distinct display directions

The [Fallen Steam Train X58001](/products/x58001-jiestar-fallen-steam-train-building-set-x58001) is listed with 2,353 pieces and a finished model size of 38.5 × 32 × 39 cm. Its official imagery presents a steam locomotive within a tall station-front setting, giving the build a vertical display profile.

![Blue steam locomotive building model on a section of track](/images/blog/new-releases/new-jiestar-trains-railway-x58001-x58002-x58003-body-01.avif "Mallard Steam Train X58002")

The [Mallard Steam Train X58002](/products/x58002-jiestar-mallard-steam-train-building-set-x58002) is listed with 1,413 pieces and a finished model size of 64.7 × 12.8 × 8.3 cm. Its long blue locomotive form and track base create a horizontal display option.

![Yellow and red electric locomotive building model with overhead line structures](/images/blog/new-releases/new-jiestar-trains-railway-x58001-x58002-x58003-body-02.avif "German 103 Electric Locomotive X58003")

The [German 103 Electric Locomotive X58003](/products/x58003-jiestar-german-103-electric-locomotive-building-set-x58003) is listed with 1,356 pieces and a finished model size of 40 × 11 × 20 cm. The official product image shows the locomotive on a display base with overhead line structures.
