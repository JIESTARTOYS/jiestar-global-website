An exhibition stand is a temporary place, but the moments inside it become part of a brand's story. Between 2023 and 2026, JIESTAR brought building block models, boxed collections, product ideas, and our team to eight fairs across Russia and Asia. Each city gave the display a different character. Together, the photographs form a journey through how we have introduced JIESTAR face to face.

The story begins in Moscow in 2023, where a full wall of products placed the breadth of our range on view at Mir Detstva. It continued in Jakarta with a colourful mix of flowers, vehicles, architecture, and street scenes. These early photographs show two different ways of presenting the same idea: JIESTAR is not defined by a single subject, but by the many creative directions that building blocks can take.

## From compact product walls to open brand spaces

Hong Kong showed how a compact booth could still carry a complete brand introduction. Booth 3E-F05 used every level of its product wall, combining finished models with packaging beneath clear company identification. Later that year, Shanghai expanded the idea into a large red-and-yellow environment with open product tables, a central brick installation, and room for our team to demonstrate models directly to visitors.

![JIESTAR booth 3E-F05 at Hong Kong Toys and Games Fair 2025](/images/blog/exhibitions/hong-kong-toys-games-fair-2025-cover.avif "A compact international showcase filled with finished models and boxed sets.")

![Large JIESTAR booth E7G32 at China Toy Expo 2025 in Shanghai](/images/blog/exhibitions/china-toy-expo-2025-shanghai-cover.avif "The Shanghai presentation introduced JIESTAR at a larger architectural scale.")

Moscow, Shanghai, and Vietnam also preserve the human rhythm of an exhibition. Their photographs show people gathering at counters, moving along the product walls, and speaking with the team. We cannot read an outcome into any one conversation, and we do not need to. The value of the images lies in seeing products become the centre of a real exchange.

## New expressions in 2026

Our 2026 stands made the development side of the business more visible. In Shenzhen, JIESTAR and iBlock appeared within a red-and-white space carrying clear OEM/ODM wording, connecting finished products with the possibility of customization and co-development. In Bangkok, the Asia Art Toy & IP Show sector created a warm setting for flowers, decorative builds, and colourful display models, with our team visibly welcoming visitors.

![JIESTAR and iBlock OEM and ODM exhibition stand in Shenzhen in 2026](/images/blog/exhibitions/toy-hobby-china-2026-shenzhen-cover.avif "A product-led Shenzhen stand with development capabilities visible in the architecture.")

![Open JIESTAR booth at the Asia Art Toy and IP Show in Bangkok](/images/blog/exhibitions/mega-show-bangkok-2026-cover.avif "Our latest stop in this journey brought JIESTAR into a creative Bangkok setting.")

Across all eight events, some things remain constant. We use real assembled models because a finished build communicates form and character immediately. We place products together because a range is more than a series of isolated boxes. Most importantly, we bring our team because exhibitions are places to listen as well as present.
