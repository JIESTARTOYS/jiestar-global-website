International toy fairs give buyers a direct way to examine finished building block models, compare packaging, understand category breadth, and begin a practical conversation with the people behind a product line. This archive brings JIESTAR's recent exhibition stories together in one place. Each entry covers one fair, uses photographs from that event, and separates the event date from the date on which the article was published.

The record currently follows eight appearances between 2023 and 2026, across Russia and major Asian sourcing markets. It is intended as a useful reference for distributors, retailers, specialist toy stores, ecommerce operators, and product-development partners preparing to meet JIESTAR.

## What buyers can learn from the archive

The photographs are not a substitute for specifications or a current quotation, but they are useful evidence of how JIESTAR presents product families in a professional sourcing environment. Across the events, visitors could review boxed ranges alongside assembled display models, see how different themes work together as a retail assortment, and identify the directions most relevant to their own channel.

A productive fair conversation usually starts with a clear market and use case. A distributor may want a balanced seasonal range, while an online retailer may prefer a focused group of display models. A custom-project partner may arrive with a concept, target customer, packaging direction, and expected quantity range. Bringing those priorities makes it easier to move from visual review to a structured follow-up.
