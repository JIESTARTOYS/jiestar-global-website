Three recent GULY additions take mechanical building in distinctly different directions. The Shadowed Wing model turns a three-headed dragon into a large display-focused mecha. The 1:8 drift car combines an aggressive automotive shape with remote-control functions. The W16 engine makes its internal structure the centre of the finished model.

All three are currently listed in the JIESTAR online catalog. The specifications below come from the supplied English product materials, while the product links were checked against the current Shopify catalog. Listing status and availability can change, so buyers should use each product page or contact JIESTAR for the latest information.

## 80508 Shadowed Wing: a three-headed dragon built for display

The [GULY Shadowed Wing Mecha Model Kit 80508](/products/guly-shadowed-wing-mecha-model-kit-80508) is the most sculptural model in the group. Its black-and-red body supports three articulated dragon necks, broad orange wings, a long segmented tail, and gold-coloured horn and wing details. A display stand helps hold the model in its upright pose.

The supplied English materials identify the set as a 3,975-piece model for ages 8 and above. Its displayed dimensions are approximately 106 cm wide, 71.2 cm deep, and 87 cm high. The same materials show movable joints, wings that can expand or fold, and light-guide details around the necks and body.

Those proportions make 80508 a statement piece rather than a compact shelf model. Collectors should consider both its height and full wingspan when planning a display area. Visually, the repeated neck structure, orange membranes, and dark mechanical body give the model a strong silhouette from the front as well as from an angle.

## 10659 Raceforge: a 1:8 remote-control drift car

The [GULY 1:8 Remote-Control Drift Stunt Car Model Kit 10659](/products/guly-1-8-remote-control-drift-stunt-car-model-kit-10659) shifts the focus from fantasy form to automotive movement. The finished model uses red body panels, black structural elements, a low cockpit, wide wheel arches, and light-guide accents to create a track-inspired profile.

According to the supplied English product sheet, the model contains 2,525 pieces and measures approximately 56 cm long, 26.4 cm wide, and 14.6 cm high. It is presented at 1:8 scale for ages 8 and above. The materials also show remote-control operation, a drift tyre setup, a lifting chassis, an opening cockpit door, engine linkage, lighting, and a spray-effect module.

This is the most function-led set of the three. It may suit builders who want a large car model that can be explored after assembly rather than remaining only a static display. Anyone comparing control functions, included power components, charging requirements, or package contents should confirm the current details on the product page before purchasing.

![Red GULY Raceforge 1:8 remote-control drift car building block model 10659](/images/blog/new-releases/new-guly-model-kits-80508-10659-60556-body-02.avif "The supplied product image highlights the low red body and exposed technical structure of model 10659.")

## 60556 W16 Engine: mechanical structure as the subject

The [GULY W16 Engine Model Kit 60556](/products/guly-w16-engine-model-kit-60556) takes a different approach by presenting an engine and transmission as the complete display model. Silver-grey pipework, transparent sections, red mechanical highlights, exposed gears, and a black base make the internal construction easy to study from several angles.

The supplied English product materials specify 2,520 pieces, an age guidance of 8 and above, and an assembled size of approximately 47 cm long, 29.2 cm wide, and 23.2 cm high. They also illustrate linked gears, connecting rods and pistons, adjustable gear movement, light elements, and a spray-effect module.

Compared with the dragon mecha and drift car, 60556 has a lower and more technical display profile. It may appeal to automotive enthusiasts, engineering-minded builders, and collectors who prefer a model with visible mechanisms rather than an exterior body shell.

![Silver and grey GULY W16 engine building block display model 60556 on a black base](/images/blog/new-releases/new-guly-model-kits-80508-10659-60556-body-03.avif "The supplied product image shows the W16 engine, transmission housing, pipework, and display base.")

## Three different ways to build around mechanics

The clearest difference between these models is not simply their size. Each uses mechanical building for a different purpose:

- 80508 prioritises dramatic scale, articulated creature design, and display presence.
- 10659 combines a recognisable supercar form with remote-control and stunt-oriented functions.
- 60556 focuses attention on engine architecture, linked movement, and technical display.

For individual collectors, the choice comes down to subject, available display space, and whether movement or sculptural presence matters more. For retailers and distributors, the trio can also support different customer groups within one mechanical building range: fantasy-mecha collectors, performance-car enthusiasts, and engineering-focused builders.
