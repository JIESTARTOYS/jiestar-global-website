Four recent JIESTAR botanical sets approach flower-inspired building from different display directions. Two focus on tall bird-of-paradise arrangements, one creates a crescent-shaped wall piece, and one brings sunflowers and tulips together in a vase. Each design uses a distinct silhouette, making this group useful for builders comparing upright, tabletop, and wall-oriented subjects.

## Two takes on bird-of-paradise flowers

The 530-piece [JIESTAR Bird of Paradise Flower Arrangement Building Set](/products/jj9268-jiestar-bird-of-paradise-flower-arrangement-building-set), SKU JJ9268, forms a 33.5 × 14.5 × 18.5 cm composition. Its public image shows orange flowers, layered dark-green leaves, pale hanging elements, and a rounded white vase.

![Orange bird-of-paradise brick flowers with broad green leaves in a white vase](/images/blog/new-releases/new-jiestar-flower-jj9268-x88056-x88052-x88053-body-01.avif "A second bird-of-paradise arrangement uses a black pedestal vase and copper-toned leaves.")

At 796 pieces, the [JIESTAR Tropical Bird of Paradise Flower Arrangement Building Set](/products/x88056-jiestar-tropical-bird-of-paradise-flower-arrangement-building-set), SKU X88056, takes a taller direction. Its stated finished size is 36 × 18.7 × 44 cm, while the product image highlights orange and purple flower details, curved green stems, copper-toned leaves, and a black pedestal-style vase.

## A floral composition designed for the wall

The [JIESTAR Golden Moonflower Wall Decor Building Set](/products/x88052-jiestar-golden-moonflower-wall-decor-building-set), SKU X88052, changes the format from a freestanding arrangement to a crescent-shaped floral display. The 939-piece model has a stated finished size of 27 × 7.5 × 36 cm. Its warm palette combines yellow, cream, orange, brown, and muted green elements across a densely layered curve.

![Crescent-shaped floral wall build filled with yellow cream and orange flowers](/images/blog/new-releases/new-jiestar-flower-jj9268-x88056-x88052-x88053-body-02.avif "The crescent composition combines several flower forms with small hanging details.")

## Sunflowers and tulips in a tall vase

SKU X88053, the [JIESTAR Sunflower and Tulip Flower Arrangement Building Set](/products/x88053-jiestar-sunflower-and-tulip-flower-arrangement-building-set), contains 739 pieces and measures 21.5 × 15.5 × 41.5 cm when finished. The public product image shows yellow sunflower heads and red tulip forms rising from a pale vase, creating a strong vertical profile.

![Yellow sunflowers and red tulip brick blooms arranged in a tall pale vase](/images/blog/new-releases/new-jiestar-flower-jj9268-x88056-x88052-x88053-body-03.avif "The arrangement pairs warm flower colours with long green stems and a light vase.")

Across the four designs, builders can compare compact and tall arrangements, a wall-oriented floral crescent, and two different vase styles. The listed piece counts, recommended age of 8+, and finished dimensions come from the current public product records; package guidance remains the place to check build difficulty.
