## Two mechanical display builds

JIESTAR has added two engine-focused building sets to the catalogue: the V8 Engine Model Building Set and the V12 Engine Model Building Set. Both are designed around the visual character of an engine layout, with layered mechanical forms presented on display bases.

The [JIESTAR V8 Engine Model Building Set](/products/33001-jiestar-v8-engine-model-building-set) is SKU 33001. Its listed specification includes 1,123 pieces, a recommended age of 14+, and a finished model size of 19.2 x 13 x 20 cm.

![Silver and red V12 engine model on a black display base](/images/blog/new-releases/new-jiestar-engine-models-33001-33002-body-01.avif)

## A V12 option for the same collection

The [JIESTAR V12 Engine Model Building Set](/products/33002-jiestar-v12-engine-model-building-set) is SKU 33002. The public listing identifies 1,079 pieces, a recommended age of 14+, and a finished model size of 19.2 x 13 x 19 cm. Its contrasting red, silver, and black elements create a different silhouette while keeping the focus on the engine form.

For collectors building a mechanical display theme, the two models offer distinct V8 and V12 subjects to consider side by side.
