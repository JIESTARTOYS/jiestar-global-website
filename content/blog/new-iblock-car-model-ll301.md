The new [iBlock Jungle Racer Off-Road Vehicle Building Set](/products/jungle-racer-off-road-vehicle-building-set) brings an off-road vehicle and a compact jungle setting into one building scene. The official imagery uses a navy, white, orange, and black vehicle palette against green landscape elements, creating a clear contrast between the vehicle and its surroundings.

The published product details list 462 pieces and an 8+ recommendation. They also list separate finished sizes for the vehicle and tree: 14.5 x 7 x 9 cm for the vehicle and 18 x 10 x 10 cm for the tree. These two elements give the build a broader display composition than a vehicle alone.

![Brick-built off-road truck with a jungle tree, waterfall, and black panther](/images/blog/new-releases/new-iblock-car-model-ll301-body-01.avif "The off-road truck is displayed with a jungle tree, waterfall, and black panther.")

## A vehicle with a jungle setting

The product images show a small waterfall and tree scene beside the vehicle, plus a black panther figure and a small explorer figure. The truck itself has large wheels, a roof-mounted camera-style element, and a compact drone-like model shown separately in the imagery. These visible details help give the model a scene-led character while keeping the vehicle at the center.

![Jungle racer vehicle shown in a forest scene with its landscape elements](/images/blog/new-releases/new-iblock-car-model-ll301-body-02.avif "The jungle racer is shown with its tree, waterfall, drone, and explorer details.")
