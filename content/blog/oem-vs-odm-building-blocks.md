OEM customization usually starts with a buyer's brand or specific customization needs. This can include packaging changes, logo application, color adjustments, or market-specific product presentation.

ODM development goes deeper. It often involves product concepts, model direction, product structure, packaging strategy, and a development process that creates a more differentiated product.

For some partners, the best path is product co-development. This allows the supplier and buyer to plan exclusive SKUs, product series, or long-term sub-brand cooperation around the partner's market strategy.
