Three new JIESTAR floral building block sets are now listed in the online catalog. Although all three are designed around flowers, they take very different approaches to decorative display: one combines a red rose with a dramatic sculptural hand, another builds a soft pink arrangement on a white pedestal, and the third pairs blue flowers and sunflowers with a cypress-inspired vase.

The current product materials identify all three sets for ages 8 and above. Their shapes, colour palettes, and finished sizes also give collectors several distinct display directions rather than three variations of the same bouquet.

## The three sets at a glance

- X88054 — 1,613 pieces, recommended for ages 8+, finished size approximately 20.8 × 18.4 × 40.2 cm
- X88055 — 820 pieces, recommended for ages 8+, finished size approximately 21.5 × 15.5 × 41.5 cm
- X88058 — 799 pieces, recommended for ages 8+, finished size approximately 30.5 × 23.9 × 35.4 cm

These figures come from the current product workbook and English product materials. Catalog status, stock, price, packaging, and availability may change, so the linked product pages remain the best reference for current shopping information.

## X88054: Eternal Vow Rose Hand Sculpture

The [JIESTAR Eternal Vow Rose Hand Sculpture Building Set](/products/x88054-jiestar-eternal-vow-rose-hand-sculpture-building-set) is the most elaborate of the three by piece count. A white skeletal hand holds a deep red rose above a circular base filled with red flowers, black thorn-like forms, dark architectural details, and restrained gold accents.

The contrast gives the set a gothic, theatrical character. Instead of presenting flowers as a conventional bouquet, the composition turns the rose into the focal point of a larger scene. The tall vertical shape and detailed base make it suitable for collectors who prefer a display model with a stronger narrative presence.

![JIESTAR X88054 Eternal Vow rose sculpture with a skeletal hand and dark floral base](/images/blog/new-releases/new-jiestar-floral-building-block-sets-x88054-x88055-x88058-body-01.avif "X88054 uses a red rose, sculptural hand, and dark circular base to create a dramatic floral display.")

## X88055: Heartfelt Bloom Flower Arrangement

The [JIESTAR Heartfelt Bloom Flower Arrangement Building Set](/products/x88055-jiestar-heartfelt-bloom-flower-arrangement-building-set) moves into a lighter and more contemporary palette. Translucent pink botanical forms rise above a white pedestal bowl, joined by a white lily-like bloom, small white flowers, green stems, and red accent details.

At approximately 41.5 cm high, it has the tallest listed finished height of the three sets while keeping a relatively compact footprint. The open spacing between the upper stems and the fuller arrangement below gives the model a clear silhouette for a shelf, desk, side table, or gift-oriented display.

![JIESTAR X88055 Heartfelt Bloom pink floral building block arrangement on a white pedestal](/images/blog/new-releases/new-jiestar-floral-building-block-sets-x88054-x88055-x88058-body-02.avif "X88055 combines translucent pink botanical forms with white flowers and a pedestal-style base.")

## X88058: Cypress Bouquet Flower Arrangement

The [JIESTAR Cypress Bouquet Flower Arrangement Building Set](/products/x88058-jiestar-cypress-bouquet-flower-arrangement-building-set) uses a wider blue-and-gold composition. Sunflowers provide bright focal points among blue, pale yellow, and white rose-like and lily-like forms, while the brown and green vase introduces a cypress-inspired vertical structure.

Its listed width of approximately 30.5 cm makes it the broadest of the three finished models. The combination of deep blue, light blue, yellow, green, and brown creates a layered display that can be viewed as both a floral arrangement and a decorative colour study.

![JIESTAR X88058 blue and yellow cypress bouquet building block arrangement](/images/blog/new-releases/new-jiestar-floral-building-block-sets-x88054-x88055-x88058-body-03.avif "X88058 brings sunflowers and layered blue blooms together above a cypress-inspired vase.")

## Choosing the right display direction

X88054 is the strongest choice for a darker, more sculptural statement. X88055 offers a softer pink arrangement with a tall, airy profile. X88058 provides the widest bouquet and the most varied blue-and-yellow colour story.

Beyond colour preference, collectors can compare the finished footprint, height, piece count, and the surrounding room where the model will be displayed. A narrow shelf may favour a more vertical design, while a wider surface can give the cypress bouquet more room to show its layered arrangement.
