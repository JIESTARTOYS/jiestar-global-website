The new iBlock Modular Fire Rescue Team Building Set, SKU LL911, brings three red rescue-themed models into one 879-piece set. The current public product images present a fire truck, a helicopter, and a seaplane, giving builders three different vehicle silhouettes within a consistent red, white, grey, and yellow colour direction.

## Three rescue vehicles in one set

The fire truck has a stated finished size of 6 × 13 × 10 cm. Its public images show a compact multi-axle body, a clear cab, yellow wheel details, and equipment mounted above the roof.

The helicopter measures 16 × 21 × 9.5 cm. The finished model uses a clear cockpit, a large top rotor, landing skids, and a red-and-white body with grey mechanical details.

The seaplane is listed at 16 × 14 × 8 cm. Its product render shows a wide red body with a clear cockpit, twin turquoise fan details, and white lower sections supporting the aircraft-style profile.

![Red brick fire truck helicopter and seaplane shown in a dramatic rescue scene](/images/blog/new-releases/new-iblock-fire-rescue-ll911-body-01.avif "The product artwork presents all three LL911 rescue vehicle builds together.")

## A closer view of the truck and helicopter

The body images provide a closer look at two of the models. On the truck, the clear front and side sections keep the cab visible, while the red body is broken up by white lower panels and yellow equipment details. The helicopter image highlights the angled cockpit, rotor assembly, tail fan, and landing structure.

![Close view of a red brick fire truck with clear cab and roof equipment](/images/blog/new-releases/new-iblock-fire-rescue-ll911-body-02.avif "The LL911 fire truck combines a clear cab with red panels and yellow equipment details.")

![Close view of a red brick rescue helicopter with rotor and landing skids](/images/blog/new-releases/new-iblock-fire-rescue-ll911-body-03.avif "The LL911 helicopter features a clear cockpit, large rotor, and red-and-white body.")

The three finished sizes make the set easy to compare as a coordinated vehicle group rather than a single large display model. The public record lists a recommended age of 8+ and directs builders to the product package for difficulty guidance.
