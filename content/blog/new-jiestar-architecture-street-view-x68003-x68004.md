## Two new rural scene builds

JIESTAR has added two farm-themed sets to its Architecture & Street View range: the Rustic Barn Farm Building Block Set and the Sunflower Farm Building Block Set. Both present a self-contained rural scene with building details, landscaping, and small character elements visible in the supplied product images.

The [JIESTAR Rustic Barn Farm Building Block Set](/products/x68003-jiestar-rustic-barn-farm-building-block-set), SKU X68003, has a listed piece count of 2,223. Its public specifications give a finished model size of 33.5 x 20.5 x 36 cm and a recommended age of 14+.

![Sunflower farm building set with orange roof and garden](/images/blog/new-releases/new-jiestar-architecture-street-view-x68003-x68004-body-01.avif)

## A sunflower-filled companion scene

The [JIESTAR Sunflower Farm Building Block Set](/products/x68004-jiestar-sunflower-farm-building-block-set), SKU X68004, is listed with 1,986 pieces and a finished model size of 31 x 25.5 x 19 cm. The model pictured in the product media combines a warm-toned house, sunflower planting, and a small blue vehicle, making it a natural companion to the barn scene.

These two releases suit collectors who enjoy architecture and street-view settings with a countryside theme.
