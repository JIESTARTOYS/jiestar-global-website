IBTE Indonesia 2024 gave JIESTAR a dedicated setting to introduce its building block presentation to buyers in Jakarta. For a retailer or distributor, a fair booth offers something a spreadsheet cannot: the ability to compare packaging, assembled models, visual themes, and the overall coherence of a product family in one visit.

The photographs in this story come from the Indonesia event archive. They have been selected separately from the later Vietnam show so that each article documents a recognisable appearance rather than reusing similar files across two fairs.

## Verified event information

IBTE Indonesia 2024 was held from August 7 to 9 at Jakarta International Expo. The [official IBTE exhibitor page for Zhejiang Guaibaobao Technology Co., Ltd.](https://ibte.co.id/exhibitor-list/zhejiang-guaibaobao-technology-co-ltd/) records the company listing and the event details.

## A display built for product comparison

The booth photograph shows a compact, clearly branded environment with boxed building block sets placed across the display. Several package sizes and model directions are visible together, allowing visitors to scan the range before focusing on individual items. This matters to buyers planning an assortment: a product has to work on its own, but it also needs to sit logically beside other SKUs online or on a retail shelf.

The photograph confirms the physical presentation at the fair, not the current commercial status of every pictured item. Product specifications, price, stock, minimum order quantity, packaging language, and delivery timing should always be checked for a new enquiry.

## What Indonesian buyers could evaluate

Indonesia is a varied retail market, so the same catalogue may be approached differently by specialist toy stores, department stores, ecommerce sellers, gifting channels, and regional distributors. At the booth, buyers could begin by identifying themes and price-position directions appropriate to their customers, then ask which items can form a coherent opening selection.

Physical review also helps with questions that are easy to overlook in a digital catalogue. Buyers can consider how much shelf space a box requires, whether the finished model communicates its appeal clearly, how multiple packages work as a family, and which products may be better suited to display-led or play-led positioning. For a deeper review, JIESTAR's [quality and safety overview](/quality-safety) provides a starting point for due-diligence questions.

## From a fair visit to a useful brief

The best follow-up begins with practical information. Buyers should share their country or region, sales channel, preferred product categories, approximate order direction, and any packaging or branding requirements. A focused brief helps separate a standard wholesale enquiry from a project that may require OEM / ODM work or product co-development.

JIESTAR's [wholesale cooperation page](/wholesale) is the route for discussing current ranges and channel supply. Partners looking for custom packaging, a new product concept, an exclusive SKU, or a longer-term product line can use [Custom Solutions](/custom-solutions). None of these options implies that a particular product, commercial term, or territory is automatically available; each project requires confirmation.
