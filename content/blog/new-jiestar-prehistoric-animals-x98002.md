The JIESTAR Mosasaurus Display Model Building Set X98002 is a new addition in the Prehistoric Animals category. Its official imagery shows a Mosasaurus model held inside a tall display enclosure with transparent panel sections.

## A large-format prehistoric display

X98002 is listed with 2,033 pieces, a recommended age of 14+, and a finished model size of 38 × 24.5 × 18 cm. The model combines the creature form with a structured display setting rather than a standalone figure.

![Front view of a Mosasaurus building model in a transparent enclosure](/images/blog/new-releases/new-jiestar-prehistoric-animals-x98002-body-01.avif "Front view of the X98002 display enclosure")

The official product visuals show a front display panel, technical-style graphics, and exposed mechanical-style details around the lower enclosure. Another image presents the model from an angled view, showing the Mosasaurus shape inside the transparent structure.
