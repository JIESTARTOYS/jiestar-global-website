# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

JIESTAR's global website serves retail building block customers, collectors, wholesale buyers, distributors, ecommerce sellers, and business partners exploring OEM / ODM customization, product co-development, exclusive product lines, or long-term sub-brand cooperation.

## Product Purpose

The site exists as an international building block brand hub that supports DTC product browsing, future Shopify checkout, B2B wholesale inquiry, custom product development inquiry, and long-term SEO content. Success means visitors quickly understand JIESTAR as a trustworthy global building block brand with clear paths to shop, request wholesale supply, or start custom cooperation.

## Brand Personality

International, premium, practical, and trustworthy. The tone should feel clean and modern without becoming childish, factory-generic, discount-heavy, or overbuilt.

## Anti-references

Avoid traditional factory websites, generic Shopify template stores, cheap dropshipping pages, crowded low-end product grids, childish toy styling, unverified certification claims, and complex custom ecommerce backend patterns in Version 1.

## Design Principles

- Lead with brand trust before decoration.
- Keep DTC shopping and B2B cooperation paths visible without splitting navigation into too many items.
- Use real product, factory, quality, and cooperation imagery wherever possible.
- Keep pages premium, clear, fast, and maintainable for a one-person team.
- Let Shopify handle commerce infrastructure while Next.js owns presentation, SEO, and experience.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use semantic HTML, proper heading hierarchy, descriptive image alt text, visible focus states, sufficient contrast, keyboard-accessible navigation, and mobile-first responsive layouts. Avoid motion or visual effects that reduce readability or performance.
