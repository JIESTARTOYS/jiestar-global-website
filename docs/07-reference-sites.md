\# JIESTAR Reference Sites



\## 1. Reference Goals



The purpose of this file is to collect reference websites for visual style, homepage structure, product pages, B2B pages, and SEO content.



JIESTAR should not copy these websites directly. The goal is to understand what makes a global building block or toy brand website feel trustworthy, premium, and conversion-oriented.



\## 2. Building Block / Toy Brand References



\### LEGO



Website:

https://www.lego.com/



What to study:

\- Brand presentation

\- Product categories

\- Product detail pages

\- Gift guide structure

\- Content and SEO structure



\### Nifeliz



Website:

https://www.nifeliz.com/



What to study:

\- Premium visual design

\- Homepage layout and section rhythm

\- Product image presentation

\- Adult collector-oriented brand feeling

\- Clean product grid

\- Color, spacing, and typography

\- Product detail page structure

\- How to make building block products feel more premium and display-worthy



What JIESTAR can learn:

\- Use stronger product visuals instead of simple product listing.

\- Make the site feel more like a lifestyle / collector brand, not just a toy store.

\- Keep the layout clean, with clear spacing and strong hero sections.

\- Present building block models as display products, not only as toys.

\- Use visual storytelling to improve perceived product value.



What JIESTAR should avoid:

\- Copying the design directly.

\- Making the brand too close to Nifeliz.

\- Over-focusing on C-end collectors while ignoring B2B cooperation.

\- Hiding wholesale, custom solutions, and sub-brand partnership too deeply.

\## 4. What JIESTAR Should Learn



\- Clear product categories

\- High-quality product visuals

\- Strong homepage first impression

\- Separate B2B and DTC paths

\- Clear inquiry buttons

\- Trust-building sections

\- Better SEO page structure

\- Professional product specifications



\## 5. What JIESTAR Should Avoid



\- Copying competitors directly

\- Overly childish design

\- Cheap discount banners

\- Too many colors

\- Messy product grids

\- Generic factory website layout

\- Weak product descriptions

\- No B2B cooperation path
