\# JIESTAR Homepage Structure



\## 1. Homepage Goal



The homepage should help users understand within a few seconds that:



\- JIESTAR is a professional international building block brand.

\- JIESTAR has factory supply capability and product development capability.

\- JIESTAR supports DTC purchases.

\- JIESTAR supports B2B wholesale supply.

\- JIESTAR supports OEM / ODM, product co-development, and sub-brand partnership.



The homepage should not be just a product listing page. It should also not feel like a traditional domestic factory website.



The homepage should serve as:



\- Brand presentation entry

\- Product entry

\- B2B wholesale entry

\- Custom Solutions entry

\- DTC shopping entry

\- SEO content entry

\- Trust-building entry
