## 4. Non-Product Website Image Shooting List

These images are not single-product white background images. They are brand, B2B, trust, and website storytelling images needed for the homepage and static pages.

### Homepage Hero

- [ ] 1 wide hero image suitable for the first screen
- Suggested content: premium building desk, several representative building block models, packaging or design sheet details, clean background
- Requirements: landscape / wide format, enough negative space for text, premium and uncluttered, no cheap promotional graphic style

### Company / Brand Image

- [ ] Office, showroom, brand wall, or team work scene
- Used for: About page and brand trust sections
- Requirements: bright, clean, international, not low-end factory style

### Factory and Production Capability

- [ ] Production workshop
- [ ] Assembly area
- [ ] Warehouse or storage area
- [ ] Packaging area
- Used for: Wholesale page and B2B trust content
- Requirements: tidy environment, organized workflow, avoid sensitive equipment details or messy backgrounds

### R&D / Product Development

- [ ] Design discussion scene
- [ ] Sample review scene
